# Safety and governance

FluHost-AA is limited to analysis of naturally occurring sequences and statistical associations. It does not design or engineer viruses or provide experimental protocols. The [project scope contract](contracts/project/scope_v1.0.yaml) states the operative research boundaries.

Scientific parameters are governed: host ontology, inclusion and exclusion rules, deduplication, QC, references and numbering, statistical tests and correction, sampling, lineages, temporal and benchmark splits, and causal terminology. Changes follow **proposal -> PI review -> contract update -> implementation**. Unknown historical F0 choices remain `TBD`; they are not replaced with modern defaults.

Public contributions must respect source data terms, avoid restricted or personal data and secrets, and keep raw inputs immutable. Claims of adaptation, transmission, pathogenicity, fitness, or immune escape require explicit cited evidence; default outputs are statistical associations. See [interpretation guidance](docs/scientific_interpretation.md) and [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md).

For any proposed GPT-Rosalind use, see the [access and safety governance requirements](docs/access_and_safety_governance.md) and [proposed computational use case](docs/gpt_rosalind_use_case.md). These documents do not assert that organizational controls or model access are already in place.
