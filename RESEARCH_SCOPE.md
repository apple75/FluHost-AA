# Research scope

FluHost-AA analyzes publicly available, naturally occurring Influenza A sequences through reproducible computational bioinformatics. The current analytical focus is HA host-associated amino-acid signals. The [frozen project scope contract](contracts/project/scope_v1.0.yaml) is authoritative for permitted and excluded work.

The planned program includes subtype-aware analysis, temporal and geographic validation, lineage and phylogeny-aware association, reassortment context across segments, and temporal or held-out ML evaluation. F0 is a separate historical reproduction phase governed by its [draft contract](contracts/science/f0_legacy_baseline_v1.0.yaml); its unresolved parameters remain `TBD`.

The project does not design new influenza viruses or reassortants, optimize transmission, pathogenicity, host range, or immune escape, or provide virus rescue, culture, infection, or adaptive passaging protocols. Computational observations concern sequences that already occur in source data. See [safety and governance](SAFETY_AND_GOVERNANCE.md) and [scientific interpretation](docs/scientific_interpretation.md).
