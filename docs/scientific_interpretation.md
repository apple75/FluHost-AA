# Scientific interpretation

The default claim is **statistical association**. A Human vs Avian amino-acid frequency difference can identify a host-associated amino-acid signal in observed sequences, subject to sampling, metadata quality, subtype, time, geography, lineage, and phylogenetic dependence. It does not by itself establish mechanism or causality.

Use precise terms such as *host-associated amino-acid signal*, *frequency difference*, *cross-subtype consistency*, *temporal stability*, *geographic stability*, *phylogenetic robustness*, and *naturally occurring variant*. These describe the evidence actually evaluated. Reassortment context or a genome constellation is contextual analysis, not proof of a causal effect.

Do not automatically state that a signal causes human adaptation, increases transmission, or increases pathogenicity. Stronger biological claims require explicit support from cited published evidence and review under the [governance rules](../SAFETY_AND_GOVERNANCE.md). The [scope contract](../contracts/project/scope_v1.0.yaml) is authoritative for interpretation boundaries.
